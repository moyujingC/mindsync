# AI FDE 英文面试口语练习与句式

> 目标：明天能清楚、从容地沟通，不追求复杂词汇或完美口音。
> 练习原则：每次只说 2 到 3 句，停下来等面试官反应。忘词时用短句重说，不要硬接长句。

## 1. 先记住这几个“救场句”

这些句子可以帮你争取思考时间，也能让英语表达更自然。

| 场景 | 可以直接说 | 中文意思 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 没听清 | `Sorry, could you please repeat the question?` | 不好意思，您可以重复一下问题吗？ |
| 想确认理解 | `If I understand correctly, you are asking about...` | 如果我理解对，您是在问…… |
| 需要一点时间 | `Let me think for a moment.` | 让我想一下。 |
| 先讲结论 | `The short answer is...` | 简短说就是…… |
| 转入例子 | `Let me use one project as an example.` | 我用一个项目举例。 |
| 不确定但愿意说明 | `I have not done that at a large scale, but I have related hands-on experience in...` | 我还没有大规模做过，但在……方面有相关实操。 |
| 英文表达卡住 | `Let me put it in a simpler way.` | 我换一种更简单的说法。 |
| 确认时间 | `Would it be okay if I take a moment to explain the context first?` | 我能先花一点时间解释背景吗？ |

## 2. 每日 20 分钟练法

1. 先读“30 秒自我介绍”三遍，不看中文也能讲出大意即可。
2. 讲两个项目，每个只讲 60 秒；用手机录音，听一遍后删掉太长的句子。
3. 从第 5 节任选 5 个问题，先看中文想 10 秒，再用英文回答 30 秒。
4. 最后把“救场句”读一遍。真实面试中忘词时先用它们稳住节奏。

发音不需要逐个单词纠正。重点是：放慢 15%，每句话后停半秒，把重音放在业务名词和动作词上，例如 `customer problem`、`workflow`、`quality check`、`delivery`。

## 3. 30 秒自我介绍：先练这个

```text
Hi, I am Xing Cui. I have a background in software engineering, consulting, and AI product work.

My strength is turning unclear business problems into practical workflows and delivery plans. Recently, I have been building AI products and using AI coding tools in a structured way.

That is why I am interested in this AI FDE role.
```

逐句意思：

- `I have a background in...`：我的背景包括……
- `My strength is...`：我的优势是……
- `turning A into B`：把 A 转成 B；这是本次最重要、最好用的句型。
- `That is why...`：这就是为什么……；用于自然收尾。

如果对方请你讲更多，再接：

```text
I started my career in software and game development. Later, I worked in consulting, where I handled complex client needs and cross-functional coordination.

In the past year, I have combined these experiences in AI product and AI-assisted delivery work.
```

## 4. 项目口语卡：每个项目先说 4 句

### 4.1 项目一：AI 曼陀罗解读产品

```text
One project is an AI mandala interpretation web MVP. It is a multimodal AI application in the psychological wellbeing domain.

It started from a real problem: manual interpretation takes a lot of time and is hard to standardize.

I turned this service into a product workflow, from artwork upload to AI reports and follow-up questions.

I also designed quality checks and safety boundaries, because it involves psychological wellbeing and self-exploration.
```

可继续展开：

```text
The AI workflow has two steps. First, we create visual grounding for the artwork. Then we use that grounding, user intent, and output rules to generate the report.

This helps us make the output more consistent and easier to evaluate.
```

关键字：

- `web MVP`：Web 最小可行产品。
- `manual interpretation`：人工解读。
- `standardize`：标准化。
- `visual grounding`：让模型先基于画面建立稳定的视觉事实依据。
- `consistent`：一致的、稳定的。
- `evaluate`：评估。

### 4.2 项目二：AI 一人公司工作系统

```text
The second project is an AI work system for my own company.

The problem was that long-running AI work can become fragmented. Important context stays in chat, and it is hard to reuse or hand over.

I built a monorepo structure for projects, agent roles, shared tools, and a knowledge base.

Before an important change, I define the goal, acceptance criteria, edge cases, and verification method. After the change, I keep QA and delivery records.
```

可继续展开：

```text
I use Codex and Claude Code for AI-assisted development. The key is not only generating code. It is giving the AI clear context, task boundaries, and a way to verify the result.
```

关键字：

- `fragmented`：零散、割裂。
- `hand over`：交接。
- `acceptance criteria`：验收标准。
- `edge cases`：边界情况、特殊情况。
- `verification method`：验证方式。
- `task boundaries`：任务边界。

### 4.3 项目三：房产咨询与服务流程沉淀

```text
Before my recent AI work, I co-founded a real-estate consulting business.

We worked with complex, high-value client decisions, so we had to understand goals, constraints, risks, and different stakeholders.

Over time, we turned repeated frontline experience into SOPs, knowledge bases, risk checklists, and training materials.

This experience taught me how to start with an unclear customer problem and turn it into an executable plan.
```

关键词：

- `co-founded`：联合创办。
- `constraints`：约束条件。
- `stakeholders`：相关方。
- `frontline experience`：一线实践经验。
- `executable plan`：可执行方案。

## 5. 常见问答练习

### Q1. Why are you interested in this role?

```text
I am interested in this role because it combines three things I enjoy: understanding real customer problems, building practical AI workflows, and making sure the solution can be delivered and adopted.

My background is across engineering and consulting, so I am comfortable translating between business needs and implementation details.
```

### Q2. What does FDE mean to you?

```text
For me, an FDE is someone who connects the customer problem, the technical solution, and the delivery result.

It means understanding the real workflow first, choosing a realistic scope, building or guiding the solution, and validating it with clear criteria.
```

### Q3. What is your experience with AI coding tools?

```text
I use Codex and Claude Code for implementation, debugging, documentation, and verification.

I do not treat AI output as automatically correct. I first clarify the task, set acceptance criteria, review the changes, and verify the result.
```

### Q4. Do you have experience with SDD?

```text
Yes, I use the core idea of specification-driven development in my workflow.

Before an important change, I clarify the expected behavior, task boundary, edge cases, and verification method. This makes AI-assisted work more controllable.

I have not yet led a large enterprise-wide rollout of a specific SDD tool, but I have used this approach in real projects.
```

### Q5. Do you have experience with testing?

```text
For important changes, I use test-first thinking. I define what should happen, what can go wrong, and how to verify the result before implementation.

The level of automated testing varies by project. I am continuing to strengthen the automated-testing side, while keeping quality checks and acceptance criteria in the workflow.
```

### Q6. What is your experience with Docker or Kubernetes?

```text
I have hands-on experience with cloud-server deployment, Linux, Nginx, domains, HTTPS, CI/CD, and troubleshooting.

My Kubernetes experience is not yet at the level of a platform engineer. I would be honest about that, and I would like to understand how deep this role expects Kubernetes knowledge to be.
```

### Q7. Tell me about a difficult problem you solved.

```text
In the AI mandala project, the difficult part was not simply generating a report. The real challenge was making the output stable, useful, and safe in a psychological wellbeing and self-exploration scenario.

I separated visual grounding from reasoning, added output rules and quality checks, and defined cases that require human judgment. This gave us a clearer and more testable workflow.
```

### Q8. What would you do when a customer request is unclear?

```text
I would not start by choosing a tool. First, I would clarify the business goal, current workflow, users, available data, constraints, and success criteria.

Then I would propose a small pilot with a clear scope, owner, timeline, and acceptance criteria. That helps us learn quickly without overcommitting.
```

### Q9. What is your biggest gap for this role?

```text
My recent AI work has been independent product development and service validation, rather than a large completed enterprise deployment.

However, I bring strong customer-facing consulting experience, engineering foundations, and hands-on AI workflow practice. I am looking for a team where I can apply these strengths to larger customer delivery scenarios.
```

### Q10. How do you work with a customer-facing team and an engineering team?

```text
I try to make assumptions explicit. With customers, I clarify the problem, priority, constraints, and expected outcome. With engineers, I turn that into a clear scope, technical questions, risks, and acceptance criteria.

Then I keep feedback flowing in both directions, so the solution stays connected to the real use case.
```

## 6. 本次最实用的语法句式

不需要记“语法名称”，记住用途和模板即可。

| 用途 | 句式 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 介绍背景 | `I have + 名词 / been + 动词ing` | `I have experience in software engineering and consulting.` / `I have been building AI products.` |
| 讲过去经历 | `I worked on...` / `I was responsible for...` | `I worked on cross-functional engineering projects.` |
| 讲职责 | `My role was to + 动词原形` | `My role was to define the workflow and acceptance criteria.` |
| 讲目的 | `I did this to + 动词原形` | `I did this to make the output more consistent.` |
| 讲因果 | `Because..., I...` / `This helped us...` | `Because it involves psychological wellbeing, I added safety boundaries.` |
| 讲流程 | `First..., then..., and finally...` | `First, I clarified the problem. Then I defined the scope.` |
| 讲能力 | `I am comfortable with + 名词 / 动词ing` | `I am comfortable with customer discovery and cross-functional communication.` |
| 讲做过与没做过 | `I have done..., but I have not yet...` | `I have done cloud deployment, but I have not yet led a Kubernetes platform team.` |
| 谨慎表达观点 | `My understanding is that...` | `My understanding is that AI coding needs clear context and verification.` |
| 说明选择 | `I chose A because B.` | `I chose a small pilot because it reduces delivery risk.` |
| 表达学习意愿 | `I am actively strengthening...` | `I am actively strengthening my automated-testing skills.` |

### 三个高频时态，只用这三个就够

- 现在事实 / 能力：`I use...`、`I work with...`、`My strength is...`
- 过去项目动作：`I designed...`、`I built...`、`I worked on...`
- 从过去持续到现在：`I have been building...`、`I have used...`

例子：

```text
I use AI coding tools in my current workflow.             # 现在习惯
I designed a two-pass AI report pipeline.                  # 过去完成的设计
I have been building the product since 2025.               # 从 2025 持续到现在
```

## 7. 不必硬说的复杂词，换成简单英文

| 如果一时想不起 | 直接换成 |
| --- | --- |
| `orchestrate` | `coordinate` / `organize` |
| `methodology` | `way of working` / `approach` |
| `granular` | `detailed` |
| `leverage` | `use` |
| `synergy` | `work well together` |
| `stakeholder alignment` | `getting the right people aligned` |
| `operationalize` | `put into daily work` |
| `robust` | `reliable` |

短句比“高级词”更可信。例如不要强行说：

```text
I operationalized a robust cross-functional methodology.
```

改成：

```text
I helped different people work with the same process, and I made the result easier to check.
```
