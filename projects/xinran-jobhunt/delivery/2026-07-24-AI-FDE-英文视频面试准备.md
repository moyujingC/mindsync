# AI FDE 英文视频面试准备

> 面试：2026-07-24 10:30，第一轮视频面试
> 岗位：AI 前线部署工程师（Forward Deployed Engineer, AI FDE）
> 原则：用清楚、自然的英语讲真实工作；工程深度如实说明已做范围，不把个人项目、方案设计或 AI 辅助开发包装成大型客户生产交付。

## 1. 开场策略

先用 90 秒版本。面试官继续追问时，再自然切入两个项目。不要从完整 18 年履历逐段开始；只保留和这份岗位相关的三条线：工程基础、复杂业务诊断、近期 AI 产品与 AI Coding 实践。

本轮特别要让对方听到这些词，但不要机械罗列：`ambiguous business problems`、`delivery workflow`、`AI-assisted development`、`specification`、`quality gates`、`acceptance criteria`、`deployment`、`customer-facing communication`。

```text
That is why this AI FDE role interests me. I enjoy working at the intersection of business discovery, AI-assisted engineering, and practical delivery. I am especially interested in helping teams move from experimenting with AI tools to repeatable workflows with clear quality and ownership.
```

## 3. 项目一：AI 曼陀罗解读 Web MVP

### 90 秒版本

```text
The first project is an AI mandala interpretation web MVP. It started from a real service problem: manual interpretation is time-consuming, difficult to standardize, and highly dependent on the practitioner.

My job was to turn that high-trust but ambiguous service into a product workflow. I defined the user journey as artwork upload, three-circle recognition, a Lite report, a Pro report, follow-up questions about the same report, and history.

On the AI side, I designed a two-pass pipeline. The first pass creates a stable visual grounding for the artwork. The second pass combines that grounding with the user intent, a topic-specific prompt pack, and output rules to generate the report. This separation reduces contradictions when one artwork is discussed from different angles.

I also designed evaluation samples, golden reports, output contracts, quality gates, and human-review boundaries. Because this is a high-trust domain, we explicitly define what the AI can generate, what needs human judgment, and what should be handed off rather than answered by the system.

The key lesson for me is that an AI solution is not just a model call. It needs a well-scoped workflow, clear output criteria, safety boundaries, and a way to validate quality over time.
```

### 面试官若问“你的技术角色到底是什么？”

```text
I am the product lead and development driver. I define the workflow, scope, report contract, evaluation logic, and acceptance criteria. I also work hands-on with AI-assisted development for implementation and integration. For deeper infrastructure or algorithm topics, I am careful to distinguish what I have personally implemented from what I have designed or validated.
```

### 可展示的证据

- 用户主路径：上传画作 -> 三圈识别 -> Lite / Pro 报告 -> 报告追问 -> 历史记录。
- 两段式报告链路：`Vision Pass`（视觉基准）与 `Reasoning Pass`（基于基准、用户意图和规则生成报告）。
- Prompt pack（提示词包）、输出契约、固定测试样本、golden reports（黄金样本报告）、debug traces（调试轨迹）和人工复核边界。
- 讲解顺序：真实痛点 -> 取舍 -> AI 工作流 -> 如何验证和控风险。

## 4. 项目二：AI 一人公司工作系统（MindSync）

### 90 秒版本

```text
The second project is an AI work system that I built for my own company. The problem was that long-running AI work easily becomes fragmented: important context stays in chat, project rules are scattered, and it is hard for either people or AI agents to reliably take over a task.

I designed a monorepo with five layers: agents, company rules, projects, shared tools, and a knowledge base. Each project has a clear entry point, and we use structured artifacts for specification, task planning, QA, delivery, and handoff.

In practice, I use Codex and Claude Code for AI-assisted implementation, and Paperclip for task collaboration. The important design choice is that AI output does not remain as an isolated chat response. It is converted into project files, verification records, and reusable knowledge when appropriate.

For example, before an important change, I define the target behavior, acceptance criteria, edge cases, and verification method. After implementation, I keep the QA result and delivery notes. This gives us a lightweight version of specification-driven development and quality control for AI-assisted work.

The result is not a finished enterprise SaaS product. It is a working internal system and a practical test bed for how AI agents, knowledge governance, and delivery workflows can work together in a traceable way.
```

### 面试官若问“这和岗位里的 SDD / Harness Engineering 有什么关系？”

```text
My practical understanding is that AI coding becomes more reliable when the model is given a stable operating environment, not only a single prompt. In my workflow, that environment includes project entry points, role instructions, specifications, task boundaries, acceptance criteria, QA records, and delivery artifacts.

I have used these ideas in real projects. I would not claim that I have rolled out a large enterprise-wide platform, but I can show how I use structured context and verification to reduce ambiguity and make AI-assisted changes more controllable.
```

### 可展示的证据

- 五层结构：`agents / company / projects / shared / knowledge-base`。
- 需求到交付链路：`spec -> task -> implementation -> QA -> delivery -> knowledge`。
- AI 协作不止是生成代码：项目入口、角色指令、任务回写、验证记录和后续复盘共同组成可接手的上下文。
- 已做的部署与排障：作品集网站云服务器部署、Linux、Nginx、域名 / HTTPS 与线上访问问题定位。

## 5. 项目三：高信任复杂服务的流程化经验

当面试官想确认客户沟通、场景发现或培训推广能力时，讲这个项目；不要主动讲得比前两个 AI 项目更长。

```text
Before my recent AI work, I co-founded a real-estate consulting business focused on complex, high-value client decisions. The work was not simply selling a product. We had to understand client goals, constraints, risks, financing, transaction process, and different stakeholders.

My role involved discovery conversations, solution design, risk control, and coordination with clients, owners, banks, renovation teams, and other partners. Over time, we turned repeated frontline experience into SOPs, knowledge bases, risk checklists, and training materials for new agents.

This is relevant to FDE work because I am comfortable starting with an unclear customer problem. I know that a useful solution has to fit the real workflow, ownership, data and risk constraints, not just look good in a demo.
```

可在被追问数据时补：

```text
With two agents and two assistants, the business generated around RMB 2 million in annual revenue. We served more than 150 high-net-worth clients, and about 70 percent came from referrals. We also reduced the ramp-up time for new agents from roughly eighteen months to six months through a structured training system.
```

## 7. 面试结束可问的问题

选 2 到 3 个即可。先问业务与交付形态，再问工具栈，不要一开始问福利或薪资。

```text
Could you share the main customer scenarios this FDE team is working on today? Are they mainly R&D-efficiency use cases, or business-process automation and AI applications in other domains?
```

```text
For this role, what is the typical split between customer discovery and solution design, hands-on implementation, and enablement or internal advocacy?
```

```text
When you evaluate success for an FDE in the first six months, what outcomes matter most: a production delivery, customer adoption, reusable solution assets, or measurable efficiency gains?
```

```text
The JD mentions AI coding, SDD, quality gates, and DevOps. What does the current engineering workflow look like, and which parts are already standardized versus still being built?
```

```text
Because this is a forward-deployed role, how often do team members work on-site with customers or travel?
```
