## 文字版 JD

Role summary:

We are hiring a Junior Forward Deployed Engineer to join our internal AI transformation team. The FDE sits at the intersection of AI deployment, product delivery, and business stakeholder engagement.

Core responsibilities:

1. Embed with business units to surface needs, translate ambiguous requests into structured opportunities, workflows diagrams, or AI feature specs.
2. Lead or co-lead POC and pilot deployments for AI Agents, RAG knowledge bases, document processing pipelines, and workflow automation.
3. Track pilot feedback and collaborate with engineering to iterate on functionality, experience, and ROI.
4. Produce functional specs, demo environments, test logs, and handover documentation.
5. Keep delivery visibility high through task trackers, risk logs, meeting notes, action items, and milestone reporting.
6. Act as the communication layer between engineering, product, business owners, and external AI vendors.
7. Contribute lightweight technical enablement work, including basic configuration or low-code builds involving LLMs, prompt engineering, RAG, agentic workflows, API integrations, and low-code tools.

Requirements:

1. 2-4 years in product, delivery, solution engineering, or project execution roles.
2. Startup, innovation, internet product, or project delivery background preferred.
3. Strong product sense, PM discipline, and technical fluency.
4. Regular use of ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor, Dify, Coze or equivalent AI tools.
5. Understand core concepts of LLM, prompt engineering, RAG, workflow, and agents.
6. Strong Chinese and English reading, writing, and meeting communication skills.

Preferred qualifications:

1. Hands-on work with AI Agents, RAG, document processing, or workflow automation.
2. ToB SaaS, enterprise systems, low-code, or consulting delivery experience.
3. Customer-facing delivery, POC, implementation, or presales support experience.
4. Basic front-end skills.
5. Exposure to overseas or English-first work environments.

What will not fit:

1. Pure product strategy profiles that avoid technical engagement.
2. People without sustained interest in AI.
3. People who require fully defined scope before acting.

## 职责拆解

- 这是一个典型的 `业务嵌入 + 试点推进 + 轻技术落地` 型初级 FDE
- 先做需求结构化，再推动 POC 和 pilot（试点）上线
- 不要求重度底层工程，但要求能看懂 AI 系统结构并做轻量配置
- 需要承担项目透明度管理和跨角色沟通

## 任职要求拆解

- 这类岗位对“能不能把模糊需求快速压成可执行试点”看得比标准 PRD 能力更重
